## Description
The Akan Name Generator is a web-based application designed to help users find their Akan names. In Akan culture, names are traditionally given based on the day of the week a person is born, along with their gender. This application allows users to select their day of birth from a dropdown menu, choose their gender, and then generates and displays the corresponding Akan name. It is a fun and educational tool for anyone interested in Akan culture or looking to explore their name possibilities
